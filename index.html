Overview

Starting from a wrist view and a task instruction, a video model generates future observations. A pose estimator stitched into the video-latent space translates these observations into camera trajectories and gripper openings.

The demonstrations span tabletop manipulation and appliance interaction on three robot platforms.

Method

An expert pose estimator supervises the stitched model during training. At inference, generated video latents provide the visual plan.

Wrist-view observation

The camera travels with the gripper, placing the interaction at the center of the visual input.

Latent-space stitching

A learned adapter connects video latents to the retained layers of a pose estimator.

Motion and grasp

Camera trajectories and gripper openings provide the outputs for downstream robot control.
