Real-World Demos

WVP evaluates 12 tasks across Franka Panda, PiPER-X, and Galaxea R1 Pro, using the same stitched pose estimator without further fine-tuning. The video generator is used directly on Franka and PiPER-X; for Galaxea, it is adapted using task-specific RGB recordings.

Abstract

Wrist Video Policy (WVP) is a closed-loop manipulation framework that combines video prediction with geometric motion estimation. Given a task instruction and current observations, a video generator predicts future wrist views. A pose estimator adapted from a pretrained 3D model recovers wrist-camera trajectories and gripper commands; a fixed hand-eye transformation maps camera poses to executable end-effector targets. Model stitching connects predicted video latents directly to intermediate pose-estimator features, bypassing RGB decoding. The resulting estimator transfers across Franka Panda, PiPER-X, and Galaxea R1 Pro without further fine-tuning, supporting zero-shot manipulation across tasks and scenes.

Method

A frozen RGB pose expert supervises the stitched pose estimator. At inference, the video generator predicts future latents, and the stitched model estimates camera trajectories and gripper openings. A fixed hand-eye transformation maps camera motion to end-effector targets; the robot executes a short horizon and replans from new observations.

Recovery from Execution Deviations

Conditioning each new prediction on the latest observation allows the robot to adjust and retry after execution errors, including a displaced block or a missed washing-machine handle.
